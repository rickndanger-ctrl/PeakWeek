Peak Week Support

Use Peak Week Coach on Mac, iPhone or iPad. Lifters follow their programs and log training in Peak Week Lifter on iPhone or iPad.

Connect a lifter

  1. In Coach, sign in with Apple and add the lifter.
  2. Open Delivery, enter their phone number, and choose Send invitation. Messages is addressed to that saved number; complete Send in the system composer.
  3. The lifter opens the invitation and continues with Apple to save the coach connection. Then they download or open Peak Week Lifter and continue with the same Apple account. They do not need to return to the text message after installing.
  4. Publish the selected training week from Program or Delivery. It appears directly in the connected Lifter app. If the program is waiting for the lifter to begin, they confirm Start Program when ready.

If your invitation still shows Download and Connect, use those steps: install or update the app, return to that invitation, and tap Connect to your coach. The invitation option remains available for older apps and existing device connections.

Already connected or using another device?

You do not need a new installation or invitation for each program. Published changes arrive in your connected app. To use another iPhone or iPad, continue with the same Apple account. If the original device is connected only by invitation, open Apple account there and link it first. Multiple training profiles can be selected explicitly; unsent results must finish sending before switching.

Training and sync

Follow the published week, tap a main lift to record your set, and optionally attach a video. Offline results stay queued on the phone and send when connectivity returns. Check My Log for the delivery state.

Connection recovery

If Apple setup is interrupted, reopen the same Safari tab and use Check setup status. If the temporary setup expired or the invitation is no longer available, ask the coach for a fresh invitation. Use the same Apple account that owns the training. Open or update Peak Week Lifter if the Open button cannot launch it. Reconnecting does not intentionally erase saved logs, and the app protects unsent results before changing profiles.

Coach control and account data

Program edits and generation remain coach actions. Sync conflicts require choosing which saved version to use. The Mac Data menu provides backup and restore. Coach Account provides sign-out and account deletion; exported files and messages are separate copies. Apple authorization is managed in your Apple account.

Delete a Lifter account

In the Lifter app, open Apple account and choose Delete Lifter account. Or open Apple account help here, even if you have not installed the app. Sign in, confirm deletion, and complete fresh Apple verification. This deletes all lifter profiles linked to that Apple account, including the coach’s copies, and revokes their device connections. A separate Coach workspace is kept. Updated connected apps clear their cached copies when online; offline devices must reconnect, and older apps must update. Exported files and messages outside Peak Week cannot be erased remotely. See the privacy policy for retention and cleanup details.

Get help

Open a support issue at Peak Week support on GitHub. For a private account or data request, ask for a private contact channel. Please include the app version, device and a description of the issue.
