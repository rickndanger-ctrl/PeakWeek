Peak Week Support

Coach your lifters on Mac and iPad, and let them follow and log their training in the iPhone companion app. App Store release is currently pending.

Connect a lifter

  1. In the coach app, sign in with Apple and use the top-left + to add a lifter.
  2. Prepare their personal invitation and share it through Messages, Mail or another sharing service. Complete Send in the system composer.
  3. The lifter downloads Peak Week Lifter, returns to the same invitation and taps Connect. They do not need a password or phone number to connect.
  4. Publish their training week from the coach app. Their workout appears on the phone.

Training and sync

Follow the published week, tap a main lift to record your set, and optionally attach a video. Offline results stay queued on the phone and send when connectivity returns. Check My Log for the delivery state.

Connection recovery

If an invitation expires or was used on another phone, the coach can prepare a new one. Reconnecting the same lifter keeps their saved workout and logs. To use a different lifter identity, contact your coach; the app protects your current training from accidental replacement.

Coach control and account data

Program edits and generation remain coach actions. Sync conflicts require choosing which saved version to use. The Mac Data menu provides backup and restore. Coach Account provides sign-out and account deletion; exported files and messages are separate copies. Apple authorization is managed in your Apple account.

Get help

Open a support issue at Peak Week support on GitHub. For a private account or data request, ask for a private contact channel. Please include the app version, device and a description of the issue.
