Peak Week Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Peak Week is operated by Richard Holguin.

Peak Week Coach lets coaches manage private lifters and training programs. Peak Week Lifter connects a lifter to their coach using a private invitation and can restore that training on iPhone and iPad through Sign in with Apple.

Information used by the apps

Coach accounts use Sign in with Apple. Lifters can use Apple sign-in in their coach’s invitation before installing the app, then sign in with that same Apple account in Peak Week Lifter to restore their training on iPhone and iPad. An existing invitation can also connect a device directly; that lifter can link Apple later. We use an Apple account identifier and verified authentication to associate the account with the invited training. We do not match accounts by email or name and do not receive your Apple password. Coaches may enter lifter names, contact addresses, optional birth years, experience and recovery information, strength references, training schedules, programs and coaching notes. Lifters may submit weights, repetitions, effort ratings, dates, notes and optional lifting videos with audio.

Purpose and access

We use this information to provide your coaching workspace, sync programs, completed days and exercise choices across connected devices, deliver invitations and collect training results for your coach. Coaches can access their own roster and submissions. A connected lifter can access only their published training and their submissions. Invitations and device credentials must remain private. Videos use private storage and temporary access links.

Cloud data is processed through Supabase. Apps also keep local copies and queues so workouts and results remain available offline. When you choose to share through Messages, Mail or another sharing service, your selected content and recipient are handled by that service.

Website setup and temporary browser storage

The invitation website uses temporary session storage in the same browser tab to preserve a short-lived setup state, a random browser proof, a nonce and an expiration. These let setup recover when you return from Apple or reload the page. This flow does not put your invitation, Apple login tokens, authorization codes, training details or profile names into browser storage. The server holds a corresponding temporary transaction and minimal completion result, which are removed by cleanup after expiration. The saved account-to-training connection remains until it is revoked or deleted. To prevent automated abuse, the server also keeps short-lived request counts and rotating, protected identifiers derived from the connection source. It does not store raw network addresses in these security records; the records expire and are removed automatically.

Apple returns a one-time authorization code in the callback web address. The page removes that address query before loading its remaining resources and does not forward it to the app or App Store. The website host may process the initial request URL in its ordinary service logs. Apple handles its own sign-in page; Supabase verifies the account and stores the coaching data. The invitation website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not add advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

No advertising or tracking

The apps do not include advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking or data sales. Peak Week does not use HealthKit or request contacts-library access.

Retention, export and deletion

Training information is retained for the active coaching relationship and workspace. Coaches can export their local Mac roster using the Data menu. Coaches can delete their Peak Week account from Coach Account settings; this removes the workspace, training records and stored videos and revokes lifter connections. App-managed copies on the device making that request are cleared. Copies on another device are subject to that device's local storage and backup controls. User-exported backups and messages already shared through another service are separate copies.

In the current Peak Week Lifter app, open Apple account and choose Delete Lifter account. You can also use Apple account help on this website and choose Delete Lifter account, including if you connected through an invitation but never installed the app. After confirmation and Apple verification, all lifter profiles linked to that Apple account are removed, including programs, training logs, coaching notes, videos, shared workout state, and the coach’s copies. All associated device connections are revoked. Updated apps clear their saved copies when they next reconnect. Offline devices must reconnect and older versions must update to clear local copies. Your separate Coach workspace, if you have one, and other lifters’ records are not deleted by this Lifter action.

Account deletion revokes the Lifter app’s Apple authorization. Video cleanup runs automatically and checks again for uploads already in progress; it normally completes within three hours and retries if the service is temporarily unavailable. Minimal deletion records containing record identifiers and hashed device credentials are retained to prevent old devices from restoring deleted data. These records contain no names, contact details, workouts, notes, or videos. User-exported files, device backups outside the app, and messages already shared through other services are separate copies that Peak Week cannot erase remotely.

Lifters without a linked Apple account, or using an older version, can ask their coach or contact Peak Week support to remove their training data. We verify requests before deleting private records. Deleting Peak Week data does not delete your Apple account or reverse an App Store purchase.

Support and privacy requests

See Peak Week support. Use the public support page to request a private contact channel rather than posting private training details, videos or invitation codes publicly.

Policy changes
